# One team, equal partners

**Rule:** Run the project as one team of managers, customers and developers who are equal partners, and let the team decide what needs doing and by whom.

**Why:** Questions, scope and schedule negotiation, and functional tests need more than developers. Customers are partners, developers contribute at any experience level, and managers focus on communication and relationships. Analysis, design, code and test are ongoing and simultaneous, not managed as phases.

**Do:**
```
one team room, all roles
assign people to work by team decision, not by phase hand-offs
managers: remove obstacles and keep communication open
```

**Don't:** Splintered groups that compete instead of cooperating, as on the troubled C3 start.

**Break it when:** The site names no exception.
