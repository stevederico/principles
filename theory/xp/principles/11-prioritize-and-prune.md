# Prioritize and prune

**Rule:** Have the customer rank stories and tear up any story that has become low value, without regret.

**Why:** Dreading the tear-up means too much time went into writing the story. A manager who changes an estimate, or a customer who refuses to prioritize, seems to take control but loses it by dodging a decision that matters. Doing the most valuable stories first means always doing as much as possible for customers and management.

**Do:**
```
each planning meeting:
    customer ranks stories by value
    drop stories that no longer earn their place
    never skip the ranking
```

**Don't:** Refusing to prioritize, or having a manager push estimates down instead of choosing.

**Break it when:** The site names no exception.

**Source:** http://www.extremeprogramming.org/lessons.html, http://www.extremeprogramming.org/rules/iterationplanning.html
