# Dedicated open work space

**Rule:** Give the team a dedicated open area with shared central computers, a stand-up zone, a table and walls for notes and charts.

**Why:** Removing barriers adds communication paths. Central computers nobody owns ease pairing and signal equal worth; perimeter desks give private work without disconnection. Visible discussions draw people with a stake, and posted progress charts inform the team without management hounding. XP projects are not quiet.

**Do:**
```
center: shared pair stations; edges: a few solo desks
add: stand-up space, conference table, whiteboards, wall for story cards
post: progress and process-improvement charts
```

**Don't:** Barriers that divide people from each other.

**Break it when:** The site names no exception.

**Source:** http://www.extremeprogramming.org/rules/space.html, http://www.extremeprogramming.org/start.html
