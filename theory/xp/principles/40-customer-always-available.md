# The customer is always available

**Rule:** Assign one or more customers who are real experts to the team, on site, so developers can ask them questions face to face throughout.

**Why:** The customer writes and prioritizes stories, negotiates release content, supplies detail when a task begins, creates functional test data, and reviews test scores to allow or stop a release. It looks like a long time to commit, but time is saved by skipping the detailed specification. Significant projects need full time. Part-time experts argue, so convene them for group meetings to settle differences.

**Do:**
```
put the expert on the team (not a trainee)
customer decides business matters: story choice, release timing, go/stop
if several customers: schedule group meetings to settle disputes
```

**Don't:** Accepting a trainee presented as an expert by the customer's department.

**Break it when:** The site names no exception.

**Source:** http://www.extremeprogramming.org/rules/customer.html
