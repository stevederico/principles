# Re-plan instead of catching up

**Rule:** Treat every plan as temporary and make a new one whenever the customer gains insight or you fall behind or get ahead, instead of trying to catch up.

**Why:** Chasing the old plan is the quickest route to falling even further behind; the right move is to stop and accept the real position. The team holds three plans: months ahead grouped into deployments, the next iteration's stories, and this iteration's tasks with sign-up. All are re-created before they expire.

**Do:**
```
on slip or early finish, or when the customer learns something:
    stop, call a planning meeting
    accept how far you have come
    write a new plan from there
```

**Don't:** Working harder to recover the old plan.

**Break it when:** The site names no exception.

**Source:** http://www.extremeprogramming.org/introduction.html, http://www.extremeprogramming.org/lessons.html, http://www.extremeprogramming.org/rules/commit.html
