# Daily stand-up meeting

**Rule:** Hold one short stand-up each morning, everyone in a circle, each person reporting what was done, what is planned, and what is blocking.

**Why:** Typical meetings waste developer time for little communication. One required short meeting replaces many others with a net saving several times its length, and lets other meetings invite only the people needed, often spontaneously at a computer where code can be browsed and ideas tried.

**Do:**
```
every morning, standing in a circle:
    each person: done yesterday / attempting today / what delays me
other meetings: invite only who is needed; hold them at a computer
```

**Don't:** Large sit-down meetings that most attendees do not contribute to.

**Break it when:** The site names no exception.

**Source:** http://www.extremeprogramming.org/rules/standupmeeting.html
