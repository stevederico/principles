# Answer new ideas with a soft no and keep no backlog

**Rule:** Reply to a raw request with "interesting, maybe someday", record nothing, and consider it again only when the need returns on its own.

**Why:** First contact is too early for yes or no. A yes commits you to something not yet understood; a growing list creates a feeling of being behind and costs grooming time. Needs that matter resurface unprompted, and an idea heard only once may not be a real problem. This also covers feedback after a release: a yes to every request removes the clean slate and acts like debt.

**Do:**
```
on new_idea(x):
    reply("interesting, maybe someday")      # neutral tone, no promise
    # no ticket, no ranking, no central list
at planning time:
    consider only fresh proposals + items someone revives with context
if the same need arrives again unprompted: shape(x)
```

**Don't:** Log and rank every request, add a feature because N people asked once, or patch in a knee-jerk reaction to loud early feedback.

**Break it when:** The item is a defect, outage, security or legal problem; those follow a separate path. A legally required defect record is a record, not planning input.
