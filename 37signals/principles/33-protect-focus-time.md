# Protect long blocks of uninterrupted time

**Rule:** Reserve contiguous hours for focused work and replace most meetings with written or lightweight exchange.

**Why:** Each interruption forces the working state to be rebuilt; a "quick" pull costs more than its length. Meetings carry little information per minute and split the day.

**Do:**
```
daily: a no-talk block of several contiguous hours
meeting only if: clear agenda, minimal invitees, hard 30-minute stop
```

**Don't:** Scatter short interruptions through the day, or lend a person "for an hour" in the middle of committed work.

**Break it when:** Incident response or work that truly needs live pairing.
