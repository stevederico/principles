# Keep the cost of change low, decide quickly, repay debt on a schedule

**Rule:** Treat the cost of changing direction as the key asset. Avoid inertia (lock-ins, permanent decisions, long roadmaps), make quick calls and revise them, ship in small chunks, and label deliberate shortcuts as debt with regular time to repay them.

**Why:** The dearer a change, the less likely it happens. Most decisions get revisited, so correction must be cheap, and paralysis hurts progress. Estimates beyond a few weeks are fantasy and small chunks are cheap to redo. Hacks are valid for shipping but accrue interest if left unlabelled.

**Do:**
```
prefer: open formats, replaceable parts, just-in-time decisions, small scope
decide(now), mark done, review when real data arrives
chunk = 6 to 10 hours ; release something small weekly
on shortcut: record(debt_item) ; each cycle: reserve a slice and repay
```

**Don't:** Lock requirements or optimise before use shows the shape, plan months with no release in them, or accumulate unlabelled hacks.

**Break it when:** A decision is irreversible (public names, money movement, data deletion, regulation); slow down there. A throwaway prototype needs no debt list.
