# Reduce the risk of not shipping before anything else

**Rule:** Target one risk at a time, and first the risk that work never finishes: shape to remove unknowns, cap each bet, integrate early. Judge a team by whether the bet shipped and felt worthwhile, not by how its hours looked.

**Why:** A good strategy is useless without the ability to act on it. Insight is wasted if you cannot turn it into shipped work, and policing daily activity undermines autonomy.

**Do:**
```
shape:  close open questions before committing
cap:    fixed time box, no default extension
build:  integrate end to end early
review: "do we feel good about what shipped after N weeks?"
```

**Don't:** Tune the discovery process while the team still cannot finish things, or audit hours as a proxy for progress.

**Break it when:** Shipping is already reliable and the main risk is building the wrong thing. If a team repeatedly misses outcomes, inspect shaping and team health, not hours.

**Source:** https://basecamp.com/shapeup/0.3-chapter-01 (Targeting risk) ; https://basecamp.com/shapeup/4.2-appendix-03 (Fix shipping first, Focus on the end result). Docs only.
