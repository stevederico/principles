# Assign the whole project, not tasks

**Rule:** Give a small integrated team the entire shaped project and its boundaries, and let them create the tasks as they learn. Most real tasks are discovered while building.

**Why:** Splitting up front shreds the concept into disconnected pieces, and plans rarely survive contact with the code. People doing the work are best placed to adjust, and task holders stop feeling responsible for how pieces fit. Early days show little visible output while people learn the system; asking for status then pushes exploration underground.

**Do:**
```
hand over: shaped concept + boundaries, not a task list
team: list imagined tasks, then add discovered tasks where they belong
days 1-3: no status requests; ask what blocks only after ~3 days of silence
```

**Don't:** Break work into tickets before anyone has touched the code, treat the first list as complete, or require ticket movement from day one.

**Break it when:** The team is new to the domain; supply a starting scope but still not a task list.

**Source:** https://basecamp.com/shapeup/3.1-chapter-10 ; https://basecamp.com/shapeup/0.3-chapter-01 (Making teams responsible). Docs only.
