# Run a shaping track one step ahead of the build track

**Rule:** Keep one track that prepares future work, privately and with at most one trusted peer, and another that builds only what has been shaped. Never hand a team a problem with no solution.

**Why:** Unshaped work is unknown and cannot be scheduled. Early shaping needs shorthand and frankness to cover many ideas fast, and is easily shelved when private; if shared widely it reads as a promise. Autonomous builders free senior time for shaping, which in turn gives builders clearer boundaries. Because the concept already names its core parts, programmers can start modelling while design explores.

**Do:**
```
cycle N:   builders build bets shaped in cycle N-1
           shapers prepare candidates for N+1, alone or with one peer
publish a candidate only when ready to ask for resources
programmer: spike the core model from the pitch; designer: explore in real templates
```

**Don't:** Push research down to builders on a deadline, or serialise design and then code.

**Break it when:** A tiny team where one person shapes and builds; the steps still exist but fit in one head.
