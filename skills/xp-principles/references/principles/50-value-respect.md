# Value: respect

**Rule:** Give and expect respect for every team member, treating all contributions, even enthusiasm, as valuable.

**Why:** Developers respect the customers' expertise and vice versa, and management respects the team's right to take responsibility and have authority over its own work. Each small success deepens respect for each person's unique contribution. This value was added to the site in 2009.

**Do:**
```
credit every contributor; ask the expert in each area
management: let the team own its work and its authority
```

**Don't:** Overriding the team's authority over its own work.

**Break it when:** The site names no exception.

**Source:** http://www.extremeprogramming.org/values.html, http://www.extremeprogramming.org/index.txt, http://www.extremeprogramming.org/recentchanges.html
