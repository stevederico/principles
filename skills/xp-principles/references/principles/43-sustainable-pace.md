# Set a sustainable pace

**Rule:** Work at a pace the team can keep for the whole project, and when you will miss a date change scope or timing instead of adding hours or people.

**Why:** Overtime saps motivation, so tired teams get less done, and overwork borrows progress from the future, which ruins realistic plans. Fred Brooks showed that adding people to a late project usually has a negative effect; if you foresee lateness, ramp up well in advance. Every team has its own perfect velocity, and pushing one team to match another lowers its velocity long term.

**Do:**
```
accept the team's velocity, guard it, plan from it
behind schedule: hold release planning, change scope or date
foresee lateness: add people slowly, well in advance
```

**Don't:** Overtime pushes, a death march, or comparing teams' velocities.

**Break it when:** The site names no exception.

**Source:** http://www.extremeprogramming.org/rules/overtime.html, http://www.extremeprogramming.org/recentchanges.html
