# Four project variables

**Rule:** Treat scope, resources, time and quality as the four variables of a project, never trade quality away, and let management set two of the other three while developers set the last.

**Why:** Changing one variable moves the others. Dropping quality below excellent has hidden effects on the rest that appear only when the project slows to a crawl before the deadline. Developers also moderate a customer's wish to finish immediately by hiring too many people at once.

**Do:**
```
quality = excellent (fixed)
management picks two of: scope, resources, time
developers set the third from their estimates and velocity
```

**Don't:** Cutting quality to hit a date, or adding many people at once to rush a release.

**Break it when:** The site names no exception.

**Source:** http://www.extremeprogramming.org/rules/planninggame.html
