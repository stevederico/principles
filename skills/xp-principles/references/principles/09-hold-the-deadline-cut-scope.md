# Hold the deadline, cut scope

**Rule:** Treat the iteration end as a firm deadline; when you will miss it, hold a planning meeting, re-estimate and remove tasks so the team finishes fewer things completely.

**Why:** Unfinished or buggy work is an unknown amount of future effort and cannot be measured. Even with one day left it is better for the whole team to focus on one finished task than many half-done ones. The customer's most important tasks should finish, not a pile of unfinished tasks the developers chose.

**Do:**
```
daily: compare progress to the iteration plan
if you will not finish all tasks:
    call iteration planning, re-estimate, remove tasks
    finish the customer's top tasks first
```

**Don't:** Leaving many tasks partly done at iteration end, or extending the iteration.

**Break it when:** The site names no exception.

**Source:** http://www.extremeprogramming.org/rules/iterative.html, http://www.extremeprogramming.org/rules/overtime.html, http://www.extremeprogramming.org/lessons.html
