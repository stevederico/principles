# Let the people who build the product answer its support

**Rule:** Rotate designers and programmers through the support inbox, reply quickly with something honest even without a fix, and put a short hint where users repeatedly get stuck.

**Why:** Numbers are not voices; hearing frustration shows why users need something, not only what. The answer comes from the person who knows the code, and a recurring question is a defect signal.

**Do:**
```
rota: everyone takes occasional support shifts ; the support team coaches on answers
first reply fast and honest ; technical issues escalate to builders
```

**Don't:** Wall developers behind a call centre or keep builders insulated from customer pain.

**Break it when:** Volume would hurt delivery (keep an escalation path), or a role handles regulated data that casual access would expose.

**Source:** https://basecamp.com/gettingreal/14.1-feel-the-pain ; https://basecamp.com/gettingreal/14.3-answer-quick ; https://github.com/basecamp/handbook/blob/main/our-rituals.md (Everyone on Support). Docs only.
